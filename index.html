Your All-in-One Platform Is Quietly Charging 4–8%.

Platforms like MindBody, Zen Planner, PushPress, and PerfectMind often advertise often base their fees on the standard 2.9% + 30¢ processor rate, but then add their own markups and extra charges. That's why gyms regularly see effective rates of 4-8% on real statements.

Common hidden costs:

  • • Processing rate markup on top of the 2.9% adn 30¢ charge from payment processors
  • • “Admin” or “convenience” fees tacked onto each transaction
  • • Gateway fees just to move money from cards to your account
  • • Payout fees every time you get a deposit
  • • Extra charges for certain card types (AmEx, rewards cards, etc.)

What that means for your gym:

  • • You think you’re paying 2.9% — you’re really paying much more.
  • • Hundreds to thousands of dollars a month leaking out in fees.
  • • Hard to compare or negotiate because you don’t “own” the processor relationship.
  • • Less cash available for coaches, mats, and growing your community.

Frequently Asked Questions

If you’re wondering what it looks like to move away from a big all-in-one platform, you’re not alone. Here are answers to the most common questions.

Will I lose my members or auto-payments when I switch?

No. The goal is a clean, safe transition. You can migrate members as their billing cycles renew, clearly communicate changes, and run both systems in parallel for a short time if needed. We’ll help you plan it so you don’t lose anyone.

Is Stripe safe and legit for gyms?

Yes. Stripe is one of the most widely used payment platforms in the world, handling billions in transactions for companies of all sizes. You get enterprise-grade security and PCI compliance with clear, published rates.

Can RollTrack App work alongside my existing software?

In many cases, yes. RollTrack App is designed as a lightweight check-in and analytics layer. You can keep some of your existing tools while using RollTrack App for attendance, retention insights, and Stripe-based billing.

How much does RollTrack App cost?

Our MVP pricing is designed so even smaller academies can benefit. A typical setup is $20 per month per gym, plus Stripe’s processing fees. The majority of gyms save money overall compared to all-in-one platforms.

Do I have to use Stripe, or can I use another processor?

Stripe is our recommended starting point because of its transparency and flexibility. If you’re already working with another processor and getting a great rate, we can discuss how RollTrack App might fit into that setup.
